Speed-to-Schedule SLA Alerts

Founder's Office take-home case for an ed-tech company. Cohort analysis of a 5,000-lead funnel showed the leak was a process gap, not rep or lead quality. The fix is a live alerting layer: warning at 24 hours without a booked demo, critical at 48, plus a daily Slack digest.

Nine-module Make.com scenario ran the full dataset with zero errors. About Rs 12 lakh a month recoverable on the case data (projection).

Make.com, Google Sheets, Slack API, Python (pandas), Claude
